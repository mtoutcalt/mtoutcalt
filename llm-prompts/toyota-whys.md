My problem: [describe your problem]

Ask me "why is that happening?" one question at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next why. Push past five rounds only if we still haven't hit bedrock.

Then give me:
1. The surface symptom I started with
2. The root cause we uncovered
3. One fix that targets the root cause, not the symptom

Rules: challenge vague answers, reject "because that's how it is," and if my answer blames a person, redirect me to the process behind them.
